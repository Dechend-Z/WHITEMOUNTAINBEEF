## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages from a new repository under the `Dechend-Z` account. Replaces the openlink.co/whitemountainbeef link page.

## Users

Primary: export and B2B buyers (importers, halal distributors, retailers, gift and food-service buyers) evaluating WHITE MOUNTAIN HALAL BEEF products, often outside Thailand, reading in English, Arabic, Malay-region English or Chinese. Secondary: Thai customers ordering by LINE or phone.

## Product Purpose

Show a buyer a credible halal processed-beef range from a real community farm in Satun and get them to contact the Export Manager.

## Positioning

Khao Khao Kho Khun Farm Community Enterprise (วิสาหกิจชุมชนเขาขาวโคขุนฟาร์ม), Ban Khao Khao, La-ngu, Satun. Families here have raised cattle for about 50 years; cattle are fed on local roughage (rice straw, coconut and palm by-products, grasses). Products are halal, made from the farm's beef with old local recipes and herbs (black pepper, turmeric, lemongrass). Satun is a UNESCO Global Geopark.

## Operating Context

Buyers arrive from a shared link, mostly on phones. Quotes and orders happen by LINE or phone; no cart.
