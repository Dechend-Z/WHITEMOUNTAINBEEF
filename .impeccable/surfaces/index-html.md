## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a hand-painted Satun kedai signboard: product names and prices lettered in several scripts side by side, as southern Muslim tea shops letter Thai, Jawi and Rumi together. Refuses the white-grid exporter page with a green halal badge row.

OWN-WORLD: Board green field (#1f5a3d family) as the page ground, cream enamel panels, signwriter red for the item on sale, mustard gold for borders and prices. One even 3px ink line around every panel, double-ruled board edges, flat fills, no gradients, no shadows. Brush-lettered display caps, plain sans for running text, tabular numerals for prices.

STORY: A buyer sees a real halal beef range from a 50-year community farm in Satun, believes it from the packaging, marks and farm story, and messages the Export Manager.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width green board.
