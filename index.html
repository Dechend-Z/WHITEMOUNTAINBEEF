    From Ban Khao Khao, La‑ngu, Satun

    Families in Ban Khao Khao have raised cattle for around fifty years, first for weddings, religious occasions and village festivals, then as a living. Today the herd is run by Khao Khao Kho Khun Farm Community Enterprise.

    The cattle eat what the district grows: rice straw, coconut and oil-palm fronds, Napier and Ruzi grass, and corn stalks. The meat goes into recipes the community has cooked for generations, with black pepper, turmeric and lemongrass.
